## Current themes

My research asks a practical question: **how can a prosthetic hand keep working well when the user, electrode position, environment, and task all change?** I approach this from both sides of the interface:

- **Intelligence:** robust sEMG regression, few-shot personalization, online adaptation, and confidence-aware learning.
- **Embodiment:** underactuated hands, compliant fingertips, passive joint mechanics, and task-based evaluation.
- **Interaction:** multimodal sensing, safe shared control, and co-adaptation between the user and the prosthesis.
