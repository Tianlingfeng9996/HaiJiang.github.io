## Collaboration topics

I am particularly interested in collaborations involving:

- time-series data segmentation and robot manipulation;
- robust and adaptive control of myoelectric prostheses;
- sEMG decoding, wearable biosignal sensing, and edge AI;
- compliant prosthetic or robotic hand mechanisms;
- multimodal grasp understanding and safe shared control;
- evaluation with standardized tasks, virtual environments, or user studies.

## For students

When contacting me about a research project, please briefly describe your background, the topic you are interested in, the methods or hardware you have used, and your expected research period. This makes it much easier to identify a concrete starting point.
